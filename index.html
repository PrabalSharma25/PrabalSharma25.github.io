About

Prabal Sharma is a Senior Data Engineering Lead at Xebia with six years of experience in solutioning and delivering cloud data platforms on Azure, Databricks and Microsoft Fabric. He builds data ecosystems that process more than 50 terabytes across 1,000+ objects from many source systems.

His work spans ETL/ELT pipelines, Lakehouse and Medallion architecture, large-scale migrations (Hive Metastore to Unity Catalog, Redshift to Fabric, SAS to Databricks, AWS Airflow to Fabric) and real-time streaming with Kafka. He has led delivery across BFSI, mining, education, gaming, retail, e-commerce, OTT and postal domains, often as Tech Lead for several projects at once.

He is a Databricks Certified Data Engineer Professional, a Databricks Partners Program member and Microsoft-certified in Fabric Data Engineering (DP-700). Based in Jaipur, he works with clients in India and internationally and is open to opportunities in Mumbai and the UAE.
